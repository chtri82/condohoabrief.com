About the Brief

What changed, what's due, and how owners, board members and managers can take part in their communities, for condos (Chapter 718) and HOAs (Chapter 720) alike.

Our standards

  • Facts first. We report what the law, the records and officials say, and we cite them.
  • Objective. We present the facts and the authorities' statements and let readers draw their own conclusions.
  • We are nonpartisan, take no position on legislation, and endorse no candidates.
  • No invented numbers. If we can't source it, we don't print it.
  • We use video only when we can confirm it is authentic and not AI-generated or manipulated.
  • Sponsor messages are clearly labeled and kept separate from coverage.

Informational, not legal advice

This newsletter summarizes Florida statutes and official sources in plain English. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Your declaration, bylaws and specific facts matter. For decisions affecting your association, consult a Florida-licensed attorney or the DBPR Division of Condominiums, Timeshares and Mobile Homes.
