About the Brief

What changed, what's due, and how owners, board members and managers can take part in their communities, for condos (Chapter 718) and HOAs (Chapter 720) alike.

Our standards

  • Primary sources first. We cite Florida Statutes Chapters 718 and 720 directly, along with DBPR and its Division of Condominiums, Timeshares and Mobile Homes, the Florida Legislature, court rulings and local building and code records, and we link to them.
  • We report what the law, the records and what officials say. We don't guess at how a rule applies to a specific association.
  • Deadlines and dates come from the statute, the agency or the court, and we name the source for each one.
  • Owners, boards, managers and developers are treated fairly. We present the facts and the officials' statements and let readers draw their own conclusions.
  • We are nonpartisan and take no position on legislation or association disputes.
  • No invented numbers. If we can't source an assessment, budget or reserve figure, we don't print it.
  • Corrections are made promptly and noted on the page.
  • We use video only when we can confirm it is authentic and not AI-generated or manipulated.
  • Sponsor messages, including from management companies, law firms and vendors, are clearly labeled and kept separate from coverage.
  • We welcome partnerships with Florida state agencies and the Legislature to serve as the go-to guide for condo and HOA owners, associations and businesses. Any such partnership will be disclosed on this page.

Informational, not legal advice

This newsletter summarizes Florida statutes and official sources in plain English. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Your declaration, bylaws and specific facts matter. For decisions affecting your association, consult a Florida-licensed attorney or the DBPR Division of Condominiums, Timeshares and Mobile Homes.
