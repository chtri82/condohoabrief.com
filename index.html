Laws & the Legislature

HB 657 died in the Senate Rules Committee on March 13, 2026. HB 797 and HB 803 took effect July 1, 2026. The 2027 regular session starts March 2, 2027.

Condo safety: SIRS & milestone inspections

If a building's milestone inspection is due on or before December 31, 2026, the SIRS must be done "in no event" later than December 31, 2026.

  • Where Florida condo & HOA law stands after the 2026 session October 20, 2026

Association websites & records

Condos with 25+ units had to post key documents online by January 1, 2026; HOAs with 100+ parcels by January 1, 2025.

  • Where Florida condo & HOA law stands after the 2026 session October 20, 2026

Board meetings & your right to speak

Owners may speak on all designated agenda items. HOA members may speak at least 3 minutes on any item at membership meetings.

Running for your board

For condo elections, your written notice of intent to run must be received 40 days before the election.

Nonprofit corporation law (Chapter 617)

HB 797 revised officer duties, created a process for courts to remove directors, and updated meeting and notice procedures.
